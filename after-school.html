After-school karate with school pick-up

For kindergarten to 5th grade. We collect your child from school, bring them to the dojo, and fill the afternoon with karate, projects and homework time until 5 PM.

How to register See tuition

2026 to 2027 school year

Registration opened on Monday, June 22. You can still send a request: places are filled first come, first served, and we’ll email you to confirm whether we can fit your child in on the days you want.

The program at a glance

A safe place for the hours after school, where your child learns the focus, respect and self-control of traditional karate alongside practical self-defense. No experience is needed.

  • Kindergarten to 5th grade

    For students entering kindergarten in fall 2026, up to 5th grade.

  • Monday to Thursday

    From school dismissal until 5 PM, following the Seattle Public Schools calendar.

  • Two to four days a week

    Every student comes at least twice a week to keep up with the curriculum. You can choose up to four.

  • Paid by the week

    Tuition is worked out weekly, so you don’t pay for school break weeks.

The $29.99 two-week trial doesn’t apply to the after-school program.

What an afternoon looks like

A typical day, from the school gate to parent pick-up.

  1. 1

    Pick-up from school

    We collect students in the dojo vans, or walk them over in a group led by our instructors.

  2. 2

    Into uniform

    Students arrive at the dojo and change into their karate uniforms.

  3. 3

    Snack

    A snack gives everyone an energy boost before training.

  4. 4

    Karate class

    Training, followed by a life-skills talk on the mat.

  5. 5

  6. 6

    Parent pick-up

    The program ends at 5 PM. Please collect your child by 5:15 PM at the latest.

More than a karate class

Our goal is to live life at the highest level, and to make the most of the after-school hours in a fun and supportive room.

  • World-class martial arts instruction, one step at a time toward black belt.
  • Bully defense and stranger awareness that build real confidence.
  • Confidence and leadership drills.
  • Daily “Mat Chats” on life skills, character and outstanding behavior.
  • Games that reinforce respect, teamwork and focus.
  • Arts, crafts and mini science projects.
  • Mini Japanese language lessons.
  • Quiet time for reading and homework.

How belt tests work

  • One at the end of every session. Each test covers the highlights of what was taught in class that session.
  • Stripes come first. To qualify, students earn all four stripes on their belt: Memory, Bronze, Silver and Gold. Each small step teaches them to set a goal and reach it.
  • Parents are welcome to watch and see what their child’s hard work has produced.
  • Missed the test? We’ll schedule a private make-up test. The fee is $30 per child.

Policies

Please read all of these before you request a place. They cover the deposit, fees, changes, cancellations, pick-up and absences.

Registration and the $50 deposit
  • An online or paper registration is a request, not an immediate booking. Once we receive it we work out whether we can fit your child in on your preferred days, then email you to confirm the spot.
  • Places are filled first come, first served, so we may not be able to offer your preferred pick-up days.
  • Once confirmed, your child’s spot is secured until the end of the school year in June 2027, unless we receive a cancellation notice ahead of time.
  • A $50 non-refundable registration deposit is required after we confirm and secure the spot. It goes toward the first month of tuition, and it isn’t processed until your child’s pick-up days are secured.
Payments and fees
  • Tuition is calculated weekly, so you don’t pay during Seattle Public Schools break weeks.
  • How you pay: by EFT directly from your bank account (please provide a voided check) or by a credit card on file, during the first week of every month.
  • Early-release Wednesdays: if your days include Wednesday, there is an extra $15 per day on top of tuition because of the early release time.
  • Sibling discount: additional siblings receive 10% off the full price listed on this page.
  • Declined payments: a payment that can’t be processed or is declined is automatically charged a $40 processing fee.
  • Equipment: every student needs a correctly sized white karate uniform (top and bottom), training gloves and a dojo cinch bag. Buy them separately, or at the discounted price of $72 plus tax in the ASP Karate Equipment Package. If you choose the package, it is charged on your child’s first day in the program.
  • Returning students: please check that every item is labeled and still fits. If a size is wrong or something is lost, we’ll contact you about buying a replacement.
Changing your child’s days
  • The program is popular, so changing days isn’t always possible. We’ll do our best, depending on availability.
  • To ask about a change, email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com. We can’t accept verbal requests.
  • Pick-up days can’t change from day to day or week to week, because our pick-up routes are planned carefully.
Cancellations and refunds
  • Send cancellation requests in writing to emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com at least 2 weeks before the date you want to withdraw. We can’t accept verbal requests, because we need the details in writing in case of any confusion.
  • For example, to withdraw from March 1, your written request needs to reach us no later than 2 weeks before March 1.
  • If we don’t receive a written request 2 weeks before the first day of the month you withdraw, that month’s tuition is charged to your account.
  • There are no fee reductions or refunds for late arrivals, early departures, classes missed through homesickness, dismissal, personal vacation or voluntary withdrawal.
  • A friend can’t take an absent student’s place. Every student must be registered to take part.
Pick-up and late pick-up
  • At registration, please list everyone who is authorized to pick up your child.
  • If someone who isn’t on that list will be collecting your child, email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com to let us know.
  • Please pick up no later than 5:15 PM. If you can’t make it by then, call us as soon as possible at 206-437-3932.
  • If we haven’t had a call by 5:15 PM and your child hasn’t been picked up, a late pick-up fee of $25 per 10 minutes is charged.
Absences and illness
  • Planned or foreseen absences (sickness, appointments, travel): email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com no later than 12 PM that day if your child doesn’t need to be picked up from school.
  • Sudden changes after 12 PM: call 206-437-3932 as soon as you know. If your child goes home sick from school, for example, tell us right away so we aren’t left waiting for them.
  • No-notice fee: $25 if we haven’t heard by email or phone before that day’s pick-up time: 2:25 PM on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday, and 1:10 PM on Wednesday.
  • One van usually collects from two schools. Waiting for a student who turns out to be absent makes us late for everyone at the next school.
  • Please don’t rely on teachers or school staff to tell us. Some schools have no one assigned to do that, so letting us know is the parent or guardian’s responsibility.

How to request a place

Requests go through Zen Planner, the dojo’s member system. Sending one doesn’t book the spot: we’ll email you to confirm your child’s days.

New students

  1. Open the sign-up page and choose the after-school program membership you want.
  2. Fill in your details and select Next.
  3. Sign the liability waiver.
  4. Complete the registration information and select Sign up.
  5. Finish by signing the after-school program membership waiver.

Returning students

  1. Open the sign-up page and choose the after-school program membership you want.
  2. Sign in to your Zen Planner account, update your details and select Continue.
  3. Check the registration information and select Sign up.
  4. Finish by signing the after-school program membership waiver.
Request a place online

The button opens Zen Planner in a new tab. When you’ve finished, use “Visit the finances page” to add or update your payment method, and “Visit your profile page” to update your information. The $50 deposit is charged to that payment method once your registration is confirmed.

Fill in the after-school program registration form and send it to emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com, mail it, or drop it off at the dojo: 8314 Greenwood Avenue N, Seattle, WA 98103.
