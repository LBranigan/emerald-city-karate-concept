Tobey Stansbury

Founder and head instructor. Go-Dan Shihan, which translates to fifth-degree black belt, professor.

Tobey Stansbury has been training in traditional karate-do since 1988. He began at the Hombu Dojo in Yakima, Washington, the headquarters school for Shudokan karate in America, and grew up there under the direct tutelage of Hanshi Morris Mack.

Mack Hanshi is famous as one of the pioneers of traditional martial arts in the Northwest. That legacy began with his own teacher, Walter Todd, who learned in Japan and went on to teach judo, aikido and karate-do in America. Tobey counts his close relationship with Mack Hanshi among his greatest treasures.

He lived in Japan for two and a half years, researching karate and teaching English in the Japanese public school system. On his return to the United States he opened the Emerald City Karate dojo. His hope is for it to be a great school for students interested in traditional karate and how it applies to real life today, physically and philosophically.

His way of teaching works for students of every age, from the youngest beginners to the most experienced adult black belts. His passion for karate is evident in every class he teaches, and it inspires his students to become better from the inside out. He believes the ultimate aim of karate is not victory or defeat but the perfection of character.
