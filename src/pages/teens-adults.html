Much more than a workout

You’ll train the traditional techniques of Shudokan karate-do next to simple, effective modern self-defense, and feel the satisfaction of improving yourself through your own effort.

  • A style with a heritage

    We teach an authentic style of karate from Okinawa, Japan. That history makes every class a cultural experience as well as exercise. Where Shudokan comes from

  • Self-defense that’s practical

    Our integrated self-defense program teaches no-nonsense techniques similar to those found in Krav Maga, so you know you could protect yourself and the people you love.

  • A stronger, looser body

    Adults in the program notice big improvements in their coordination, strength and flexibility.

Calm in the middle of a hectic week

Classes include breathing exercises and short meditation sessions that relieve stress and tension, and bring the world back into focus and balance.
