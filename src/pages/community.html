Charities and non-profits

We strive to be a pillar of excellence in our community. Here is where that effort goes.

  • Local schools

    We are proud to support and sponsor the Greenwood Elementary Jog-a-thon, and school auctions for nearly every local school in the area.

  • Hunger Goblin

    We support the annual Hunger Goblin drive every October.

  • Greenwood Classic Car Show

    We support the car show each June.

  • Working families

    Our transported after-school program serves families in our community. About the after-school program

  • Home-school families

    We offer special daytime classes for families who dedicate their lives to educating their own children.

  • Parents’ Night Out

    A monthly dojo event with Nerf games, dodgeball, foam sword battles, pizza parties and more. About Parents’ Night Out

School auctions, jog-a-thons and more

A $1,000 college scholarship for black belts

Emerald City Karate is part of the American Shudokan Association, which has given more than $200,000 to karate students graduating and moving on to higher education.

The scholarship is available to every actively training black belt who graduates from high school with at least a 3.0 grade point average. Tobey Sensei and many other members of our organization have received it.

It is part of our dedication to our students’ futures, and it sets us apart from other martial arts organizations. Train hard now, because all of that sweat will pay off when you apply to your favorite university.

  • $1,000 toward college
  • More than $200,000 awarded so far
  • For actively training black belts
  • High school GPA of 3.0 or higher

Demonstrations and seminars

Our dedication to the community goes beyond our regular classes. Invite us to your school, event or workplace.

  • Karate demonstrations

    We have been honored to perform at several schools and community events. A performance brings pulse-pounding martial arts, a short history of this timeless art, and a brief mat chat on the values and philosophy of self-mastery.

  • Self-defense seminars

    For small groups, businesses and corporations, adapted to each group’s situation. Topics range from simple preparedness to putting self-defense concepts into practice. We run a Stranger Danger program for children, and a program for women with honest, straightforward self-defense options.
