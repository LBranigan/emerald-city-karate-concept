Parking, drop-off and pick-up

Please park legally when you drop off or pick up, and don’t wait in front of the dojo with the engine running.

A car double-parked on Greenwood blocks the bike lane and pushes traffic into the turning lane, which makes the crosswalk at 84th dangerous.

  • Our reserved lot. The dojo has dedicated parking just north of the building.
  • Street parking. Along Greenwood Avenue and the neighborhood streets around it.
  • Loading zones. For quick drop-offs and pick-ups.

Current students: the full drop-off and pick-up procedure is on the student hub.

What can we help with?

  • Starting karateSign up for the two-week trial online in about three minutes.
  • Questions before you startWhat to wear, how often to come, and whether it’s safe.
  • Trouble logging inContact us rather than making a second account, and we’ll issue you a password.
  • After-school programKarate after school, with pick-up from local elementary schools.
  • Summer campsDates, themes, rates and registration.
  • Donations, demos and seminarsAuction requests, school demonstrations and self-defense seminars.

Transferring from another dojo or style? Email us and we’ll arrange a private meetup and assessment to find the right class for you.
