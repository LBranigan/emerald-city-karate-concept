Bring a buddy

Training is even more fun with friends. A Buddy Pass lets a friend of one of our students come and try a class for free.

Use a Buddy Pass Four short steps, then you’re on the mat

What to expect

A Buddy Pass is a chance for friends of our students to come and try a class for themselves, with nothing to buy and nothing to bring.

  • Come as you are. Buddy Pass students don’t need a uniform. Comfortable clothes are all it takes.
  • We’ll lend you a white belt. So you feel like part of the dojo from the first minute.
  • Made for first-timers. Classes are beginner-friendly, high-energy, and a great way to see what karate is all about.

Four steps to your free class

  1. 1

    Scan the pass

    The QR code on your Buddy Pass brings you to this page. If you’re reading this, that one is done.

  2. 2

    Fill out the form

    The Buddy Pass form below tells us who is coming to class.

  3. 3

    Complete the waiver

    We’ll email it to you. Please fill it out before the day of your class.

  4. 4

    Book a class and come train

    Pick a class on our calendar, then come down to the dojo and start.
