Introduction to Karate-do

The ground breaking words of Tōyama Kanken (1888-1966) are presented here for the first time ever in English.

Tōyama Kanken was the assistant instructor of Itosu Ankō Sensei, who first brought karate out from under the veil of secrecy in Okinawa. One of only three known masters to have studied under both Itosu Ankō and Higashionna Kanryo, Tōyama Sensei is an unsung hero of the ancient martial arts. Ever diligent, Tōyama Sensei continued his budo education by studying the martial arts of Taiwan, then opened his dojo called the “Shūdōkan” upon settling in Tokyo.

His expertise has affected countless martial artists and directly contributed to the founding of several important divergent styles. From among the ranks of Tōyama Sensei’s Shūdōkan and its affiliated dojos sprang masters of Shūdōkan, Doshinkan, Yoshinkan, Keishinkan, Koeikan, Hakushinkai, Tsuchiya-ryu, Toyama-ryu, Renbukan, Shito-ryu, Shotokan, Kyokushin, Goju-ryu and many others. His first-hand knowledge of the grandfathers of karate is unprecedented and will surely deepen the understanding of any serious karate student.

“Introduction to Karate-do: Its Inner Techniques and Secret Arts” contains the information from not just one but multiple publications of this captivating text by Tōyama Kanken. As it features pictures from different books at different points in Tōyama Sensei’s life, you will be able to compare his art as it progressed in time. This book is extremely rare even in its native Japanese language so it is sure to make a treasured addition to any collection of important books on the Okinawan art of karate-do.

Featuring

  • The origins of karate
  • The moral code of karate-do
  • His thoughts on styles and factions
  • Detailed explanations of karate’s foundational skills
  • How to use and make a makiwara punching post
  • Kata: Naifanchi Shodan and Koryu Gojushiho
  • Kumite: partner training drills
  • The transcript of a round table discussion of Okinawan karate masters
  • Itosu Sensei’s dying instructions
  • Inner techniques and secret skills including: the double style breath method, the lion’s method, the tiger’s method, methods for a strong grip, methods to train the eyes, the bear claw technique, and the triangle jump

About the translator

Tobey Stansbury has trained in Shudokan karate-do since 1988, having started at the age of seven. The life changing lessons of the martial arts immediately had a huge impact on his life, as it cured him of a severe childhood stutter. Having dedicated his life to the martial arts, Tobey relocated to Japan in 2005 to research Toyama Sensei’s karate and train under as many of his students as possible. After returning from Japan, he opened a dojo in Seattle, Washington where he continues to pass on the traditions of karate-do to the next generation.
