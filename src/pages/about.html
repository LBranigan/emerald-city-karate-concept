An international journey to strengthen one community

Emerald City Karate was founded by Tobey Stansbury, who dedicated his life to the martial arts at seven years old.

His goal was a positive place where students learn an effective self-defense system and, with it, a complete program of personal growth. We get there by teaching life skills in every class, to foster integrity, goal setting, academic excellence, focus and respect.

The community has welcomed and supported us in return.

Greenwood, today
The dojo moved to the Phinney Neighborhood Center, then to Fremont, and then to our current home on Greenwood Avenue. With each move, the one constant has been our dedication to our students and to improving their lives.

What makes us different

It’s rare to find an authentic martial arts school these days. It’s rarer still to find one run by an instructor who studied and lived in the country the art comes from.

Emerald City Karate has direct ties to Japan and to the masters living there. You’ll learn an authentic form of the art, in an atmosphere of excitement, discipline and fun.

Life skills, not only confidence

Many families look to the martial arts to build their children’s confidence. Our Life Skills program goes further, teaching respect, focus, teamwork and perseverance.

We teach them in a mat chat every day and throughout the drills in class. The mat chats are often inspired by timeless Japanese proverbs, called kotowaza and koan. Two classics:

  • 猿も木から落ちる Saru mo ki kara ochiru Even monkeys fall from trees.
  • 七転び八起き Nana korobi, ya oki Fall down seven times, get up eight.

The whole family can train here

Our program is serious enough for adults and fun enough for children. Most of our members train as part of a family: moms and dads, brothers and sisters, all on the mat together.

Parents can train side by side with their child, separately in the adults-only program, or mix the two with one class a week of each.

Remember: the family that kicks together, sticks together.
