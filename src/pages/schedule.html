Good to know

  • Every class is a family class

    Parents are welcome to join their children in any class, and family discounts are available. In Little Dragons, parents train free. How family karate works

  • Adults have extra options

    As well as the Teens & Adults class, adults can attend any white and color belt class.

  • Striped belts follow their main color

    If your belt has a stripe, go to the class for its main color. The All colors classes are for gold through green belts.

  • Reserve every class

    Class sizes are capped so everyone gets attention. Book in Zen Planner; when a class is full we may ask you to come to another. How reserving works
