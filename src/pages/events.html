Parents’ Night Out

Looking for a night out without the kids? We throw a party at the dojo so you can see a movie, or just spend some time together.

The evening is fun and educational for every level, from beginners to advanced: martial arts techniques, NERF blaster games, foam sword battles, dodgeball and teamwork-building exercises, in a safe atmosphere with positive role models. No experience is needed, and we provide pizza, so you don’t need to feed the kids first.

  • Ages 6 and older

    All guests must be toilet trained.

  • 5:30 to 8:30 PM

    Drop-off starts at 5:15 PM and pick-up starts at 8:15 PM.

  • From $30 per child

    $30 for active students and $40 for everyone else, with a lower rate for siblings.

  • Pizza included

    Tell us about allergies beforehand. We can order a gluten-free pizza with notice.

The active student rate is for Emerald City Karate students with an active monthly membership. Students on the two-week trial aren’t eligible for it.

Please pick up on time. If your child hasn’t been picked up by 8:30 PM, there is a late fee of $15 for every 5 minutes.

What to bring, and what to know

  • A water bottle.
  • A NERF blaster, if you have one. We provide the darts, so please don’t bring your own. If you don’t have a blaster, or your household doesn’t allow them, tell us before the event: we have a limited number to lend.
  • Activewear. Dress to move, and leave valuables at home.
  • Allergies and special needs. Email us before the event. We order the pizza in advance and can add a gluten-free one, but we need to hear from you first. For other dietary needs you may need to send food along.

How to register

  1. Open the sign-up page and find Parents’ Night Out under Events.
  2. Select Sign up and read the information for the event.
  3. Fill in your personal information.
  4. Choose the correct price for each child.
  5. Enter your payment information and sign the event waiver.

We’ll get in touch if anything is missing or the wrong fee was selected. If we don’t hear back promptly, you may lose the spot.

Buddy Week and belt presentations

Are you an active Emerald City Karate student? Invite your friends to the dojo.

Buddy Week runs all through belt presentation week, the week after belt tests. Each instructor presents new belts at the end of their first class of the week, and we show off our test material and play games. It’s a great way for your friends to see you receive your next belt, and parents are welcome to join the celebration.

Buddy pass cards are at the front desk. Pick some up and invite your friends.

Before your friend can join a class, their parent needs to fill out a waiver. Download it below and get it to us before the class your friend plans to attend.
