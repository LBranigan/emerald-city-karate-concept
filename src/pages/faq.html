Getting started

My family wants to start karate. How do we get started?

The best way to start is our unlimited two-week trial. It’s $29.99, you can come to as many classes as you like for two weeks, and it includes a dojo T-shirt and a white belt.

Start your two-week trial

Why take the two-week trial?

It’s designed to introduce you to Emerald City Karate. Over the two weeks you’ll learn fun and exciting karate techniques, get familiar with the etiquette of karate, and see how training here works. Then you can make the decision for yourself, with first-hand information.

Can we start at any time?

Yes. Every starter class takes new students all year.

  • Ages 4 to 6 join Little Dragons. Students need to have turned 4, and parents are welcome to join the class to support a shy youngster.
  • Ages 7 and older join Beginners, with the other white belts.
  • Ages 12 and older join the Teens & Adults classes, which are open to all ranks.

For ages 7 and up, belt tests are held in January, May and September, and new students need at least two months of training before their first one.

How many classes can we come to during the trial?

As many as you like; the trial is unlimited. We recommend once a week for ages 4 to 6 and twice a week for ages 7 and older.

Can the trial start on a later date?

Yes. Choose any first class in the next three weeks when you sign up, and your two weeks start that day. If you need a date further out, call or email and we’ll set it for you.

We’ve trained at another dojo or in another style. Where do we start?

Email us at emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com and we’ll schedule a private meetup and assessment to find the right class for you.

How do we sign up for a monthly membership after the trial?

Stop by the front desk and talk to our receptionist, who will set up a membership with you. Monthly memberships can only be started in person, and nothing is charged automatically when your trial ends.

Your first class

What should we wear?

Loose, comfortable clothes that are easy to move in. Sweats and a T-shirt are ideal. Shoes and socks come off for class.

You’ll get your dojo T-shirt and white belt at the front desk on your first day. Please wear the T-shirt as your uniform during the trial. A karate uniform, called a gi, can be bought or ordered at any time.

Do we need to reserve every class?

Yes, reserving your spot is required. Each class can only take a set number of students safely. We always try to fit in students who haven’t reserved, but if there isn’t enough space we may ask you to come to a different class or on a different day.

Is there a sheet for learning one to ten in Japanese?

Yes. Karate was developed and refined in Okinawa, a small island that is now part of Japan, and the first step to becoming a martial artist is understanding the culture of your dojo. Here that means learning a few Japanese words.

We count in Japanese for warm-ups, stretches and basics throughout every class, so learn your numbers and you’ll walk into your first class feeling confident.

  • Practice counting on this site
  • Open the counting sheet to print at home
  • Watch the dojo’s counting video on YouTube
Why do people bow when they enter and leave the training area?

The bow is a symbol of respect in Asian cultures. It is just like a Western handshake.

My child is shy. Can I join in?

Yes. In Little Dragons, parents are welcome on the mat for the whole class or just to hold pads and cheer, at no charge. From age 7, parents can train in the same class as their kids.

Training

How many classes a week should we attend?

Little Dragons, ages 4 to 6: we encourage one 30-minute class a week. Twice a week is an option too.

Youth and adults, ages 7 and older: we recommend two classes a week, plus some practice time in between. That gives you time to work on techniques and become confident with them before new ones are introduced.

What is kata?

Kata is a choreographed series of blocks, kicks and punches. The patterns are like dances that have been passed down from generation to generation.

In kata, students learn to challenge themselves and discover that their only enemy is self-doubt. By practicing proper basics, kata improves coordination, concentration and confidence.

How do stripes and belt tests work?

Students earn four stripes on their belt, called Memory, Bronze, Silver and Gold. With all four they are eligible for the next belt test. Tests are held three times a year, in January, May and September.

Safety and behavior

Will karate make my child overly aggressive?

Karate students are actually more controlled because of their training. Children here are taught that the martial arts are not to be used in aggression, but to defend yourself only in the most extreme situations.

We teach conflict resolution and self-control so students learn to keep their cool in stressful situations. Aggressive behavior is channeled into assertiveness, which raises a child’s self-esteem, self-discipline and self-confidence, and builds character.

Will my classes be safe? What are the chances of getting injured?

Injuries are very uncommon at Emerald City Karate, because a qualified black belt instructor teaches and supervises each class with safety in mind. Protective gear is required while sparring to reduce the chance of injury even more.

However, students must fully understand that taking part in karate could result in injuries. Students who choose to train must accept all elements of risk involved.
