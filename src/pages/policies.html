Snow days

  • We follow the winter weather policy of Seattle Public Schools. If Seattle Public Schools close, we close.
  • After overnight snow, we email weather closures through our software and post them on our website and social media pages as soon as possible.

Required equipment

Please talk to us before buying a uniform or gloves online. Students have accidentally bought the wrong style or quality, and we don’t want you to have a bad experience finding the right gear for your family.

Little Dragons, ages 4 to 6

  • White karate uniform and karate belt

Every Little Dragons student buys a white karate uniform when signing up for a membership. The uniform and belt must be brought to class in order to take part. If you lose a piece of your set, you can buy a new set at the front desk.

Youth, teens, adults and after-school students, ages 7 and older

  • White karate uniform and karate belt
  • Sparring gloves, in an approved style only

Every karate student buys a white karate uniform and sparring gloves when signing up for a membership. The uniform, sparring gloves and belt must be brought to class in order to take part. If you lose a piece of your set, you can buy a new set at the front desk, or we can order a replacement for you.

Orange belts and higher

We highly recommend a mid to heavyweight uniform once you reach orange belt. We recommend Mugen brand uniforms, which are good quality and quite affordable, and a big step up from a beginner’s uniform.

A better uniform helps your techniques look and sound (snap!) their best. That more professional look is especially preferred at tournaments, major seminars and training camps. The heavier material also lasts longer and holds up better against the grabs and pulls of higher-level training.

Registration

The two-week trial

New students can sign up for the two-week trial online or in person at the front desk. Signing up includes signing our waiver and agreement sheet, which you can do online before you come to class or on an iPad at the front desk.

Start a two-week trial

Monthly memberships

Monthly karate memberships can only be started in person at the front desk. They are not available online. Signing up includes signing our waiver and agreement sheet, which can only be done on an iPad at the front desk.

For more about memberships, talk to us at the dojo or email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com.

Tuition

Payment options and obligations

Any plan not paid in full must be paid by automatic EFT bank transfer or automatic credit card payment. Payment is taken each month according to the agreement, regardless of attendance and without pro-rating.

Transfers and refunds

A member who is unable to use their membership may transfer it to an immediate family member. There are no refunds for tuition.

Renewal

Memberships renew automatically unless 14 days’ written notice is given before the renewal date. Once renewed, a membership runs until completion. If you do not wish to renew, contact our office by email or in writing at least two weeks (14 days) before the renewal date.

Make-up classes

If you miss a class, you are welcome to attend another class for your rank and age to make up for it.

Please reserve your spot in the make-up class through the Zen Planner member app or by logging in on our website. The app gives the most seamless experience, so we recommend downloading it to your phone.

Freezing a membership

Freezing lets a member take time off from karate, for a vacation for example. Full credit for the frozen months is added to the end of your membership. Prior notice is necessary.

  • Up to 2 months may be frozen under a six-month membership, and up to 3 months under a twelve-month membership.
  • Credit is only applied if notice of the freeze is given before the month to be frozen.
