Three ways to train as a family

Most of our members train here as part of a family: moms and dads, brothers and sisters, all on the mat. Every class on the schedule is a family class, so how you do it is up to you.

  • Side by side

    Join your child’s class and learn the same lessons at the same time. Parents are welcome in any class their children are in.

  • Separately

    The kids train in their class and you train with other adults in the Teens & Adults program.

  • Some of each

    Plenty of parents train once a week with their child and once a week with the adults.

The family that kicks together, sticks together

Karate teaches what children need to do well at school and what adults need in their working lives. Learning it together brings a family closer.

Time on the mat together is a chance to build common ground with your children, and a stronger family with it.

If your children are 4 to 6, you can join them on the mat in Little Dragons, for the whole class or just to hold pads and cheer.

The same goals for everyone

Learning to set a goal and reach it is one of our specialties. Students in the Youth & Family program work toward four stripes on their belts, and each one is a quiz on the way to the next belt test.

  1. 1

    Memory

    The culture and language of karate-do. White belts learn to count from one to ten in Japanese.

  2. 2

    Bronze

    A foundational stance and a defensive block, because karate is for protecting and perfecting yourself.

  3. 3

    Silver

    Control of basic strikes and kicks, and knowing when it is right, and wrong, to use them.

  4. 4

    Gold

    The kata taught that session: a traditional pattern of techniques passed down through generations.

Along the way everyone learns the same thing: never give up. More about stripes and belt tests
