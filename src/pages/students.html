Reserve every evening class

A reservation is required for our evening karate classes. Please reserve your spot, or your children’s, in Zen Planner before you come.

Reservations let us manage the size of each class and keep everyone safe. We know it’s easy to forget, and we do our best to make room on the mat for students who haven’t reserved. But each class can only safely take a set number of students, so please reserve ahead of time so we don’t have to send anyone home.

Thank you for understanding and for your cooperation.

Belt stripes

Four stripes stand between you and your next belt test. Each one checks a different part of what you’ve learned.

  1. 1

    Memory

    Nihongo: Japanese words

    The language and culture of karate-do. For white belts, that means counting from one to ten in Japanese.

  2. 2

    Bronze

    Kihon waza: basic techniques

    A foundational stance and a defensive blocking technique.

  3. 3

    Silver

    Kougeki waza: attacks

    Control and mastery of your basic strikes and kicks.

  4. 4

    Gold

    Kata: formal military patterns

    The kata being taught in that session.

White belts: practice counting to ten in Japanese for your Memory stripe.

Belt promotions

Belt testing is one of our most exciting events. Students train hard to improve their skills and advance in rank, and learn what it takes to set a worthy goal and achieve it.

White Gold Orange Blue Purple Green Sand Brown Black

  • Who can test

    • Students who have earned all four stripes: Memory, Bronze, Silver and Gold.
    • New students need at least two months of training.
    • Existing students must not have missed more than one month of training since their last exam.

  • When tests happen

    Promotion testing is scheduled three times a year, in January, May and September. Testing forms with more details are available before each test.

    Black belt judges give students their results right after the test. A student who misses the test takes a make-up belt test or a private make-up test.

  • Belt presentations and Buddy Week

    New belts are awarded the week after Belt Test Week. Each instructor presents belts at the end of their first class of the week, and parents are welcome to join the celebration.

    That same week is Buddy Week. We show off our test material, play awesome games and present belts, so bring your buddies to the dojo all week long. A friend’s parent or guardian needs to fill out a waiver before class. Email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com to get one.

Parking, pick-up and drop-off

The safety of our students and our community is our highest priority. Please park legally when you drop off or pick up, and don’t wait in front of the dojo with your car running.

Cars double-parked on Greenwood block the bike lane. That creates a hazard for people on bikes, makes other cars swerve into the turning lanes, and makes the crosswalk at 84th dangerous.

The map shows your options around the dojo:

  • Our dedicated, reserved parking lot
  • Street parking
  • Loading zones
  • Neighborhood parking

Contact us with any questions or concerns. Thank you for helping us keep drop-offs and pick-ups safe.

How to log in

Existing customers. Log in with the email address you gave when you signed our waiver. To set your password, choose “forgot your password” on the login page and create one.

New customers. If you have never been to Emerald City Karate and have never given us your email address, create your account with the Sign up now link.

Trouble logging in?

If you have been to the dojo before and given us your email address, please contact us and we’ll issue you a password. Please don’t try a different email address: that creates a duplicate account in our software.

Call 206-437-3932 or email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com. We’re here to help.
