Summer camps

Five themed weeks of karate, NERF battles, foam swords and field trips, for ages 6½ and up. No experience needed.

See the five camps Rates

The 2026 camps have finished

Registration opened on Monday, February 2 at noon, and the last camp ran from August 17 to 21. Everything on this page describes the 2026 season: the weeks, the rates and what campers need to bring.

Camp at a glance

Each camp is fun and educational for every level, from first-timers to advanced students, with games, activities and crafts built around the week’s theme.

  • Ages 6½ and up

    For campers entering 1st grade in fall 2026, or older. You’ll have a blast even if you’ve never trained a day in martial arts.

  • 9 AM to 4 PM, Monday to Friday

    Drop-off is from 8:45 AM and pick-up from 3:45 PM.

  • $350 or $380 a week

    $350 for active students and $380 for everyone else, with 10% off for siblings.

  • Field trip included

    Transportation and field trip fees are part of the price, and every camper gets a free Emerald City Karate T-shirt.

How a camp week runs

Every day mixes martial arts with themed games, crafts, NERF blaster games and foam sword battles. The games teach teamwork, focus, control, honesty and respect.

  • Park trips. A short walk to Alice Ball Park, next to the Greenwood Library, to play games on the grass when the weather allows.
  • Tuesday is movie day. A film on the dojo’s big screen with free popcorn. The front desk runs a small snack and drink bar where everything costs $1.
  • Thursday is the field trip. Campers travel in the Emerald City Karate van to Family Fun Center or Round 1.
  • An awards ceremony closes the week, celebrating what each camper did.

  • Family Fun Center, Tukwila

    Secret Agent, Jedi Training and Ninja camps. Go-karts, laser tag, carnival rides, mini golf, billiards, batting cages, bumper cars, obstacle courses and arcades.

    Family Fun Center’s website

  • Round 1, Tukwila

    Super Hero and Wizards Wars camps. All-you-can-play video games, bowling, billiards and karaoke rooms. Campers need socks to rent bowling shoes, and the group package includes a free cup of ice cream, so please tell us about any allergies.

    Round 1’s website

Before camp

What to pack, when to arrive, and the policies you agree to when you register.

What to bring

Food and drink

  • A healthy lunch
  • Two snacks, one for the morning and one for the afternoon. We set aside two breaks of 15 to 20 minutes for campers to rest and eat.
  • A water bottle

Clothing and shoes

  • Play clothes for the weather. Shorts or pants are best.
  • Closed-toe shoes that are comfortable to walk in
  • Socks for Round 1 field trips, for the bowling shoes
  • The dojo T-shirt we give each camper, worn on field trips so the group is easy to spot. We can’t lend T-shirts because of health guidelines, so a camper who forgets theirs will need to buy a new one at the front desk.

Other items

  • Sunscreen, enough for the whole week
  • A backpack for everything
  • A NERF blaster. We provide the darts, so please don’t bring your own. If NERF blasters aren’t allowed at home, tell us: we have a limited number to lend.
  • A booster seat on Thursday, if your child is under 8 years old or shorter than 4 feet 9 inches

Each camper gets a personal cube for their belongings. Please label everything. Our staff don’t provide food, and we aren’t liable for items lost, stolen or damaged during camp.

Drop-off and pick-up

Drop off between 8:45 and 9 AM, and pick up between 3:45 and 4 PM.

Leaving early or arriving late? Call 206-437-3932 or email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com to let our staff know.

If your child is sick or will miss a day

It’s the parent’s responsibility to tell us when a child won’t be at camp for all or part of a day. Email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com or call 206-437-3932.

There are no refunds for days or weeks a camper can’t attend, and a friend can’t take an absent camper’s place. Every camper must be registered to take part.

Camp code
  • Respect the surrounding environment and the dojo by following the rules at all times.
  • Respect the counselors and everyone else, both in our group and the people we meet along the way.
  • Respect yourself.
  • Have fun!
Safety policy
  • Campers’ safety is our highest priority. Our counselors work hard to keep an environment where everyone can take part safely.
  • Occasionally a camper has a minor injury during an activity. It’s rare, and when it happens a counselor uses their first aid training to take care of them. First aid kits are within easy reach of every counselor and staff member.
  • In the unlikely event of a serious injury, we call 9-1-1 immediately and notify a parent or guardian. In a natural or other disaster, campers stay under the direct care and supervision of our staff until an authorized person checks them out.
  • Minor behavior problems are handled on the spot, with a verbal reminder or a request for an apology, for example. Repeated minor offenses lead to more formal steps, such as contacting a parent or guardian or asking for a conference.
  • Parents are notified of major behavior problems. Physically endangering others or oneself, property damage and theft can lead to a short-term suspension.
  • Campers found with illegal objects or substances, such as drugs, alcohol, tobacco or weapons, are suspended and removed from camp immediately.
Refunds and cancellations

Camp fees are refundable when you send written notice of cancellation by email. There are three tiers:

  • Cancel by May 31, 2026, 11:59 PM Pacific: full refund.
  • Cancel between June 1, 2026 and 14 days before your camp starts: 50% refund.
  • Cancel less than 14 days before your camp starts: no refund.

The $50 non-refundable deposit isn’t included in any refund.

  • No fee reductions are made for late arrivals or early departures.
  • No refunds are given for homesickness, dismissal, suspension or voluntary withdrawal.
  • If a camper has to go home for medical or other reasons, a prorated part of the fee may be refunded at Emerald City Karate’s discretion.

To cancel, email emeraldcitykaratestaff@gmail.com with your first and last name, the camper’s first and last name, and the camp session you signed up for.

Field trip transportation

Campers go on supervised off-site trips during the week, either on foot or in the Emerald City Karate van. Many trips and transportation arrangements are time-sensitive, so please make sure your camper arrives on time each day.
